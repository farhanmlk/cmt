About Conference Management Tool (CMT)

CMT is a centralized university platform designed to manage the complete conference lifecycle, including conference publishing, author registration, paper submission, verification, payment, review tracking and reporting.

Built for Lok Jagruti Kendra University (LJ University) and maintained by the IEEE Student Branch, LJ University, CMT brings every department's conferences — past, current and upcoming — onto one platform so authors, reviewers and administrators can collaborate seamlessly.

For Authors

Register once, submit papers with an instant Abstract ID, and track review status in real time.

For Departments

Organize and publish conferences by department, with brochures, deadlines and speakers.

For Administrators

Manage submissions, verify authors, track payments and generate reports from one dashboard.
